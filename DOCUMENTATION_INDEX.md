### For English Speakers:
1. Start: **QUICKSTART.txt** (5 minutes)

5. Reference: **SKILL.md** for all operation details
6. Optimize: **CONFIG_GUIDE.md** for customization
7. Learn: **USAGE_TRACKING_GUIDE.md** for pattern analysis
